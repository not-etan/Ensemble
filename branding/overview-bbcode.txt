[size=6][b]🛡️ Keystone Command[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]⚔️ Command the key. ⚔️[/b][/size]

[b]Learn the dungeon. Build the group. Make the plan. Command the key.[/b]

Keystone Command is a Mythic+ companion addon that helps players of every skill level understand each dungeon, build stronger groups and walk into every key with a plan everyone can follow. It brings group building, dungeon knowledge and planning together in one place, so you spend less time checking guides and more time playing the game.

[b]Who it's for:[/b]

[list]
[*]🌱 [b]Newer players[/b] get the dungeon knowledge that usually takes a stack of guides to piece together.
[*]🪜 [b]Players pushing higher[/b] learn the mechanics and roles that matter on the way to +10 and beyond.
[*]🏆 [b]Experienced players[/b] get the same answers in seconds, before a good applicant moves on.
[/list]

🎮 [b]Game version:[/b] World of Warcraft Retail, [i]Midnight[/i] Season 2 (patch 12.1.5).

🔄 [b]Kept current:[/b] dungeon data is checked against Blizzard's hotfixes. Last checked: patch 12.1.5 and hotfixes through October 13, 2026. The Dungeon tab shows this date too.



[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]✨ Why use it[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*]⚡ [b]Decide fast.[/b] See at a glance whether your group is ready and who would fix what's missing.
[*]📚 [b]Skip the guide-checking.[/b] Every dungeon's must-kick casts, must-dispel debuffs and must-stop channels are built in, linked to the classes that can handle them.
[*]🎓 [b]Learn as you go.[/b] Short explanations show why a group needs something, not just that it does.
[*]🗺️ [b]Walk in with a plan.[/b] Kicks, dispels and Bloodlust timing are worked out before the first pull.
[/list]



[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]🧰 Features[/color][/b][/size]

[size=4][b]📊 Composition[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🧭 [b]Group check:[/b] a summary of what your group has, what's thin and what's missing, with one color-coded chip per key need.
[*]🔎 [b]Every gap explained:[/b] who covers it now, which dungeon casts need it, and which classes could cover it with which ability.
[*]🏷️ [b]Slot badges:[/b] what each player brings, with a marker on anyone who's the group's only source of something.
[*]🌟 [b]Raid buffs:[/b] the buffs your group brings and how well each suits it.
[*]🧠 [b]Talent-aware:[/b] abilities that depend on an optional talent are marked, so you know what's guaranteed.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🧩 Group building[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🛠️ [b]Planner or live party:[/b] plan a comp from scratch, or let the slots fill with your real group.
[*]👆 [b]Spec preview:[/b] hover a spec to see what it would add or cost before you pick it.
[*]💡 [b]Suggestions for open slots:[/b] the best-fit classes for each empty slot, with a short reason for each.
[*]💾 [b]Saved comps:[/b] save, load and share comps with an export string.
[/list]

[size=4][b]📥 Group Finder applicants[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🚦 [b]Fit rating:[/b] every applicant rated Great, Good, Fair or Poor with a score out of 100, based on how well their spec fits your group and key.
[*]✅ [b]Group after:[/b] a plain line saying whether your group is ready once they join, or what's still missing.
[*]🧗 [b]Experience:[/b] their best run of this dungeon this season counts too, more so at higher keys.
[*]🧾 [b]Why this score?[/b] A point-by-point breakdown of every rating.
[*]⚖️ [b]Compare, sort and filter:[/b] pin two applicants side by side, sort by fit, rating or item level, and hide Poor fits.
[*]📨 [b]Invite from the card[/b], with a compact panel beside the Group Finder and ratings in Blizzard's own tooltip.
[*]📝 [b]"What we need":[/b] a ready-made line for your listing, such as "LF 2 DPS. Need: Poison dispel, Brez."
[/list]

[size=4][b]🔎 Groups you could join[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🏷️ [b]Your fit on every listing:[/b] a marker on each Mythic+ group showing how strong a pick you'd be.
[*]💬 [b]Details on hover:[/b] what you'd add and what the group would still need after you join.
[*]✍️ [b]A line for your Sign Up note:[/b] your spec, rating, best run of that dungeon and what you'd bring, ready to copy.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🏰 Dungeons and affixes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🗺️ [b]Every Season 2 dungeon:[/b] Altar of Fangs, Murder Row, Den of Nalorakk, The Blinding Vale, Voidscar Arena, Ruby Life Pools, Kings' Rest and Temple of Sethraliss.
[*]📖 [b]Walkthrough:[/b] every trash section and boss in order, with what each mechanic does and how to answer it, filtered by role if you like.
[*]🥇 [b]Best key:[/b] rank every dungeon by how well your current group covers it.
[*]📅 [b]This week's affixes:[/b] read from the game and included in every check and plan.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🗝️ The plan[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🎯 [b]Your assignments:[/b] your kicks, dispels and backups in one place.
[*]⏱️ [b]Kick and dispel plan:[/b] rotations shared out by cooldown, with backups for dangerous casts and a "kick first" order. Click any assignment to change who takes it.
[*]🥁 [b]Bloodlust timing:[/b] a sensible default for every dungeon that you can move, annotate or add to.
[*]🧭 [b]Pull by pull:[/b] see each pull's kicks and dispels and who takes them.
[*]🔗 [b]Route support:[/b] the plan and walkthrough follow your Mythic Dungeon Tools route. Not affiliated with MDT, so its updates may briefly break this.
[*]💬 [b]Post to party chat:[/b] the whole plan, one line per player, or just the gaps, in one click.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🐉 Boss cards[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🪧 [b]A card for every boss:[/b] short notes for everyone plus separate tank, healer and DPS lines.
[*]🪟 [b]Boss window:[/b] opens when you enter the dungeon, follows your progress and highlights your own assignments.
[*]✏️ [b]Make them yours:[/b] resize, lock, fade in combat, rewrite any card or post it to chat.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🎓 Learning and history[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]💡 [b]Learning mode:[/b] plain-language explanations throughout, or switch it off for a compact expert view.
[*]📚 [b]Glossary and tour:[/b] every term one click away, plus a quick tour on first open.
[*]📈 [b]Run history:[/b] your keys saved locally, with timed rates with and without each gap and a takeaway from every run.
[/list]

[size=4][b]🎨 Customization[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]↔️ [b]Resizable window[/b] with adjustable text size and accent colors.
[*]🎚️ [b]Choose what to check:[/b] turn off needs you don't care about.
[*]🎛️ [b]Learning and Expert presets[/b] to set everything up in one click.
[*]🌍 [b]Works on any client language.[/b] Checks use the game's IDs, though the addon's own text is English only.
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]📦 Installation[/color][/b][/size]

🔌 [b]With an addon manager:[/b] search for [i]Keystone Command[/i] and install it.

🛠️ [b]Manually:[/b] extract the archive and copy the [font=Courier New]KeystoneCommand[/font] folder into [font=Courier New]World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/[/font], then restart the game.



[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]🔒 Privacy[/color][/b][/size]

Everything runs inside the game. There's no companion app, no web service and no external data. Your comps, settings and run history stay in your own SavedVariables file, and [font=Courier New]/kc report[/font] only builds text for you to copy.



[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]❓ FAQ[/color][/b][/size]

🔍 [b]Does it use Raider.IO or Warcraft Logs data?[/b]
No. It doesn't pull live data from other sites or addons.

📚 [b]Where does the information come from?[/b]
The current patch's guides, mainly [b]Wowhead[/b] and [b]Method.gg[/b], with boss cards also checked against [b]Icy Veins[/b], [b]Raider.IO's[/b] written boss guides and the game itself. It's all built into the addon.

🏆 [b]I'm an experienced player. Is this for me?[/b]
Yes. Turn off learning mode for a compact view and you still get instant applicant ratings, a cooldown-aware plan and history insights.



[size=5][b][color=#7EB7FF]🐞 Found a problem?[/color][/b][/size]

Type [b][font=Courier New]/kc report[/font][/b] in game (or use Settings › Data). Fill in the first few lines, copy the window with Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C, and paste it in a comment here, with a 📸 screenshot if it's visual. For wrong ability or dungeon info, mention where you saw the correct version and I'll check it against the current patch.
